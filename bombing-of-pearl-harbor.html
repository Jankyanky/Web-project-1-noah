Bombing of Pearl Harbor

Prelude to the attack

In the late 1930's American forgeign policy in the Pacific wanted to support China against Japanese expansion. Which would bring Japan into conflict with the United States early as 1931 the Tokyo goverment had extended it's control over Manchuria, in the following year the Japanese made a puppet state Manchukuo. Beginning of open warfare between Japan and the United Front of Chinese Nationalists and the Chinese Communists party,the response that the United States goverment extended it's first loan to China in 1938. July 1939 the United States announced the termination of the 1911 Treaty of Commerce and Navigation with Japan and in the beginning of summer in 1940 the United Staes restricted the exports to Japan of marterial that would help them in the war. Between June 1940 and December 1941 the tension between the United States and Japan increased. Once Japan joined the Axis the US goverment cut all commerical and financial relations with Japan also freezing all Japanese assets. Japan tryed to negotiate with the United States up to the day of the attack but when it wasn't working the prime minster Tōjō Hideki declared war.

The Attack

On December 7, 1941, the Japanese launched a surprise attack on Pearl Harbor in Hawaii. The first dive bomber appeared at 7:55 am it was a part of the the first wave that consisted of nearly 200 aircraft.
