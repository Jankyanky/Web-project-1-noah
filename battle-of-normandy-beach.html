Battle of Normandy beach

the battle of Normandy beach was the largest amphibious invasion in history. It was conducted by the Allied forces against Nazi Germany in June 6 1944 the allied forces was the United states of America,Canada and United Kingdom. The Normdandy landing consisted of five beaches The five beaches that were invaded was Utah, Omaha, Gold, Juno and Sword.

Utah Beach

Utah Beach was the westernmost of the five beaches of the five during in Normandy. It was invaded by the United States 4th Infantry divison. The beach was 3 miles wide and was the least defended of the five. The United States 4th infantry division landed with about 21,000-23,000 troops and out of the 21,000-23,000 only 197-590 died that were on the beach. The beach was able to be secured by 6:30 am and the United States 4th infantry division was able to advance.

Sword Beach

Sword Beach was the easternmost of the five beaches of the five during in Normandy. It was invaded by the United Kingdom's 3rd Infantry Division with French and British commandos. The beach was about 5-miles long the germans fortifed the beach with light defenses, beach obstacles and fortified emplacements in the sand dunes. The Germans set up 75-mm guns by the beach and 88-mm guns on the cliffs. The United Kingdom's 3rd Infantry Division landed with about 29,000 troops and out of the 29,000 roughly 1,300 total casualties either killed wounded or missing, the beach was able to be secured by 10:00 am and link up with the British 6th Airborne Division that was dropped behind enemy lines.

Gold Beach

Gold Beach was the middle beach out of the five. It was invaded by the United Kingdom's 50th Infantry Division. The beach was about 5-miles long and the Germans forces consited of elements of the 716th division and some parts of the 1st Battlalion of the excellent 352nd division. Thankfully for the British the Germans armour on the beach and infantry points resistance was rendered ineffective by the British naval bombardment. The united Kingdom's 50th Infantry Division landed with about 25,000 troops and out of the 25,000 troops they suffered about 400 casualties and was able to hook up with Canadian forces from Juno.

Juno Beach

Juno Beach was the second beach from the east among the five beaches. It was invaded by the Canadian 3rd Infantry Division that took heavy casualties in the first wave of the landing. The beach was about 6 miles wide but the problem of the beach was not the germans but the beach itself because of natural offshore reefs and shoals(*funny note it was later discovered that the shoals turns out to just be seaweed*) which made it the reason and was set later then the other beaches so the landing craft could clear the reef.
